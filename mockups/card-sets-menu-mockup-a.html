Why this layout

Prioritizes scan speed and one-thumb interaction. Search and chips stay near top; high-frequency actions are always visible at the bottom.

Each set row gives context without opening another panel: name, counts, completion, and visibility toggle all in one line.

On mobile this drawer can become full-width and still keep filters plus toggles accessible without excessive scrolling.
