Why this layout

This one is mobile-first: a bottom-sheet style panel with tabs and a dense tile grid that still works on desktop.

It supports progressive complexity:

Browse tab for everyday use, Saved Views for user-defined presets, Set Health for maintenance and troubleshooting.

The footer keeps high-frequency actions in a thumb-friendly location. On small screens it behaves like an app panel rather than a long form.
